# Reply tone (Lab 15) — Grace's notes, rough

be friendly but don't gush. answer the actual question first. refunds:
quote the policy exactly (7+ days full, 3-6 days half or transfer, under 2
days nothing). NEVER say a class is allergen-free, we handle nuts etc. if
someone has a nut allergy and wants macarons, say no kindly and suggest
cookies or bread instead + offer a call. corporate = give the 8-24 range
and say I will send a quote personally. no made-up discounts, only
early-bird 10% and bring-a-friend 5%. sign off as "Grace, Cook & Bake".
spam = ignore.
