# Sample enquiries for Lab 14

Send each of these to yourself from a second address (or ask a classmate), with the subject line shown. Each tests a different part of the reply skill.

## 1. Subject: Nut allergy and macarons

Hi, my daughter is 16 and has a tree-nut allergy. Can she join the Macaron Masterclass if you use a nut-free recipe? — Mrs Lim

## 2. Subject: Refund request

I signed up for Italian Cuisine Mastery starting 7 Oct but I have to travel for work. It is 5 days before the start. Can I get my money back? — Arjun

## 3. Subject: Corporate booking

We are a team of 18 from a fintech company looking for a team-building cooking session in November, ideally Thai food. What would it cost? — Rachel, People Ops

## 4. Subject: Which course for a total beginner?

I have never baked anything. I want to make bread for my family on weekends. Which course should I start with?

## 5. Subject: Discount?

Can I get 30% off if I sign up for three courses? My friend said you do bundle deals.

## 6. Subject: SEO services — spam

Dear website owner, we can put your site on page 1 of Google guaranteed. Reply for our price list.
