# Market report — required sections

Use these section headings, in this order, for the @Sites report.

1. Summary — five bullets a busy investor reads in 30 seconds.
2. Customers — three personas: who, age, motive, when they book.
3. Competitors — a table of at least 5 named schools: course types,
   published price range, location, source link, date checked.
4. Our fees against the market — where each of our 20 courses sits
   (below / within / above the market range).
5. Demand and seasonality — search, social and calendar signals.
6. Launch line-up — the 5 courses to lead with, and why.
7. Risks — the three that could sink the first year.
8. Sources — every link, with the date accessed.

Rules: every figure carries a source and a date. Unknown = UNKNOWN.
