# Cook & Bake Academy — the founder's brief

**Founder:** Grace Lim, former hotel pastry chef (12 years).
**Date:** late August 2026.

## The idea
Hands-on cooking and baking courses for home cooks, hobby bakers and
career changers, taught by working chefs in classes of 12.

## What is already committed
- Two teaching kitchens on 3-year leases:
  - Orchard Road Bakehouse — all baking courses.
  - Bukit Timah Culinary Campus — all cooking courses.
- Seven freelance chefs, paid per session.
- A draft catalogue of 20 courses, S$160 to S$1,580 (courses.json).
- First term starts Saturday 10 October 2026.

## What Grace does not know
- Whether Singapore home cooks will pay these fees.
- Which competitors she is up against, and what they charge.
- Which 5 courses to lead with in marketing.
- Whether weekday evenings or weekends sell better.

## Constraints
- Six months of runway. No agency, no developer, no marketing team.
- Personal data must be handled under Singapore's PDPA.
