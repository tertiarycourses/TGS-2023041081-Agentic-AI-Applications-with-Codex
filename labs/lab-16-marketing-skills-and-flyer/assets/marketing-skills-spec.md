# Marketing skills to create (Lab 16)

Create each with $skill-creator as a project skill in .agents/skills/.
Every skill follows brand-voice and takes facts only from courses.csv or
term-highlights.md — never invented.

## brand-voice
Use when writing or designing anything for Cook & Bake.
Holds: the tagline, voice rules (3 do, 3 don't), banned words, colours
with hex codes, type, photo style.

## flyer-design
Use when asked for a flyer, poster, social graphic or story.
Steps: read the brief → list the must-carry items → generate with
$imagegen at the requested size (A5 portrait, 1:1, 9:16) → save the
image and the prompt used → list every word of text on the image for
proofreading. Print: 10mm quiet margin, text never over a busy area.

## social-post
Use when asked for an Instagram, Facebook or TikTok post or caption.
Hands back: hook (under 10 words) · caption under 120 words · one call
to action · 5 hashtags (3 topical, 2 local) · alt text. Never more than
1 in 5 posts is a hard sell.

## youtube-package
Use when asked to plan a YouTube video or Short.
Hands back: script with timings · storyboard (8–10 frames, made with
$imagegen) · shot list · title under 60 characters · description with
chapters and links · 10 tags · thumbnail concept (4 words max) ·
30-second Short cut-down.
