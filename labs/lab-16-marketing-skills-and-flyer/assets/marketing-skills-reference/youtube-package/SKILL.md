# youtube-package

## Hands back
- Script with timings (hook in the first 5 seconds).
- Storyboard: 8–10 frames, generated with $imagegen.
- Shot list for filming on a phone.
- Title under 60 characters; description with chapters and links.
- 10 tags; thumbnail concept with 4 words max.
- A 30-second Short cut-down.

## Never
Never plan around Sora — it is discontinued.
