# social-post

## Hands back
- Hook: under 10 words.
- Caption: under 120 words, in $brand-voice, one call to action.
- Hashtags: 5 (3 topical, 2 local such as #sgbakes).
- Alt text for the image.

## Rules
Facts only from courses.csv. No more than 1 in 5 posts is a hard sell.
Never show a learner's face without consent.
