# brand-voice

## Voice
Warm, practical, confident; a chef who likes teaching. Short sentences.
Do: say what they will make · name the chef · be specific.
Don't: hype words (amazing, world-class) · stock smiles · invented offers.

## Look
Colours: crust #B5652D · crust dark #8F4B1F · ink #2B211B ·
cream #FBF7F1 · basil #3F6B4A. Headings in Georgia. Real food, natural
light, hands at work, plenty of cream space.

## Facts
Fees, dates and courses come only from courses.json. Discounts: only
early-bird 10% and bring-a-friend 5%.
