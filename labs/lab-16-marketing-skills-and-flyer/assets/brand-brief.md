# Brand brief (Lab 16)

**Who we are:** hands-on cooking and baking taught by working chefs,
classes of 12, two campuses in Singapore.
**Promise:** you leave with real skills and the food you made.
**Personality:** warm, practical, confident, a little playful.
**Audience:** home cooks and bakers 28–45; career changers; gift buyers.
**Current colours:** crust #B5652D, ink #2B211B, cream #FBF7F1,
basil #3F6B4A. Keep them unless there is a strong reason.
**Don't:** stock smiles, clip art, hype words, discounts we do not offer.
