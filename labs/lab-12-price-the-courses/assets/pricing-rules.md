# Pricing rules (Lab 12)

- **Direct cost per seat** = (ingredients per learner per session ×
  sessions) + consumables + (chef rate + assistant wage) × sessions ÷
  learners.
- Price for **10 learners** (the planning class size; maximum is 12).
- **Target:** fee ≥ direct cost per seat ÷ (1 − 0.45) — a 45% gross margin
  covers rent and overheads.
- Flag a course **underpriced** when its fee is below that target.
- Break-even: seats per month so gross margin covers the monthly fixed
  costs in fixed-costs.csv.
- Early-bird (10%) and bring-a-friend (5%) discounts come out of margin;
  assume 20% of learners use one.
