# Enrolment email guidelines (Lab 14)

- One email per learner. Never CC, never a group send.
- Greet by first name. Under 150 words. Warm, plain, no marketing.
- Joining email (paid): course, first session date and time, campus
  address, what to bring, how to reschedule (reply to this email).
- Payment reminder (unpaid): course, amount, PayNow or card, the date
  the seat is released (7 days before start).
- Facts only from signups.csv, the Term 4 calendar and the brochures.
- Flag, do not email: missing fields; an allergy that conflicts with the
  course's allergens (Grace calls those learners herself).
- Every address in the fixture must be yours: replace YOUR.NAME with your
  Gmail username; plus-addressing (you+amelia@gmail.com) delivers to you.
