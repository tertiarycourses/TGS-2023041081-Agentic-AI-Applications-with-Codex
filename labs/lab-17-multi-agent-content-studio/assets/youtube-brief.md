# YouTube brief (Lab 17)

**Video:** "Your first sourdough loaf — 3 things our students get wrong"
**Length:** about 90 seconds, plus a 30-second Short cut-down.
**Audience:** home bakers who have tried and failed at least once.
**Host:** Chef Daniel Koh (BAK-101), at the Orchard Road Bakehouse.
**Structure:** hook (0–5s) · three mistakes with the fix for each ·
call to action: the free starter guide and BAK-101.
**Deliverables:** script with timings · storyboard (8–10 frames) · shot
list · title (under 60 characters) · description with chapters and
links · 10 tags · thumbnail concept and text (4 words max).
**Production:** film the shot list on a phone in the kitchen, or cut the
storyboard frames (made with $imagegen) with a voice-over in any video
editor. Sora is discontinued — do not plan around it.
